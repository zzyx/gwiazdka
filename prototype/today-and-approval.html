Today and approval screens

A rough, clickable prototype for the child's Today screen and the parent's approval screen. Each screen has three layouts. Switch between them with the bar at the bottom (or ← →). Everything responds: check off Tasks as the child, then switch to the parent and approve them. Children, Tasks and balances are sample data.

What to react to

  1. Child's Today: which layout fits your children? Mixing is fine ("header from A, list from B").
  2. Look: which colours and way of drawing Stars? Sunny board, calm checklist, or night sky.
  3. PLN on tap: tap the Star balance. Is that the right amount of hiding?
  4. Yesterday: how the still-open previous day is reached (try the Thursday and Saturday scenarios).
  5. Parent: which approval layout lets you clear the day fastest, and still approve a Task the child forgot to tick?
  6. Language: which one the children and you see.
