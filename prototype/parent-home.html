  • Home replaces today's Inbox at /. Day cards go away: Check-offs waiting show as blue • cells in the week grid, with All per day and a waiting count per child.
  • Weeks are newest first. This week and last week open; older weeks fold to one line with a bar per day and open on tap.
  • The Weekly bonus is decided on the week itself (No / +3 from Friday), replacing the separate week cards and the "All weeks and bonuses" fold.
  • Rules unchanged: tap cycles empty or waiting → ✓, ✓ → ✕, ✕ → ✓; All never overturns a ✕; future days can't be set.
  • /history stays for paid-out Contracts and weeks before this Contract. Contracts and Connect phone stay one tap away.
  • The day photo shows as a small thumbnail above the day's column (left out of this prototype).
