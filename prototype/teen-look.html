Gwiazdki for teens: four looks for Today

A clickable prototype for the child's Today screen, restyled for ages 13 to 17. All four keep the Sticker board's layout and rules: the Star balance (tap it for money), the Mon–Fri week strip, the day's Tasks and the notes. Only the look changes. Tap Tasks to check them off, tap the week strip to open another day, and use the bottom bar (or ← →) to switch looks.
