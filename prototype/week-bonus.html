  • When: a week can be judged from its Friday on. Before that it says "decided after Friday".
  • Grant or No bonus: both are a decision, like an Approval. Until one is made the week shows as waiting, for both of you. Either can be changed while the Contract is open.
  • Amount: always the Contract's Weekly bonus (Kuba +3, Ola +2), all or nothing.
  • Perfect week (every Task counted) is only a hint. You still decide.
  • Waiting Check-offs don't block a grant; the card says how many are left.
  • Balance and Payout include granted bonuses; a Payout lists them as bonus gwiazdki.
