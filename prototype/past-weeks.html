How does a teen look back at past weeks?

Three ways to browse earlier weeks from the Midnight Today screen (#30). Each goes back as far as there's history and stops at this week. Past days are read-only, except yesterday until 22:00, same as now.

Layout
Theme

Same in all three

  • No looking ahead: the strip's future days stay locked and you can't go past this week.
  • Summer (no Contract): those weeks still show, with a note that nothing counted then.
  • Last Contract: its weeks say they were paid out on 26 June.
  • Week total under the Tasks: gwiazdki from Tasks, plus a Weekly bonus slot that fills in once #29 lets your parent grant it.
  • A past Task you didn't do reads Not done (today it says "Not done yet").

Sample data: Kuba on Wednesday 30 September, 3 Tasks. This Contract started Tuesday 1 September; the last one ran 4 May to 26 June and was paid out. No Contract over the summer.
