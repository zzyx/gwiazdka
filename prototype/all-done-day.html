How does a finished day stand out?

Three looks for a day in Kuba's week strip once all its Tasks are done (#28), plus what happens on Today the moment the last one is checked off. Tap Tasks on Wednesday to finish the day, then let the parent approve.

Same in all three

  • All done means every Task of the day is checked off or counted. A day with a Not counted Task never lights up (last Wednesday), and neither does one with a Task left undone (last Friday).
  • Two stages: when everything is checked off but some wait for the parent, the day gets an outline. Once every Task is counted, it's filled.
  • Past weeks light up the same way, so a good week reads at a glance (try ‹).
  • Future days and days outside a Contract never light up.
  • The Moment setting only affects today, when its last Task is checked off: Off (just the strip changes), Burst (the day pops, stars fly, a short note), or Card (a card above the Tasks for as long as the day stays done).

Sample data: Kuba on Wednesday 30 September at 17:40, 3 Tasks. Monday is fully counted, Tuesday is done but Homework waits for a check, today has one Task checked off.
