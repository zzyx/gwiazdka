Contract and Payout screens

A rough, clickable prototype of the parent's Contract and Payout screens, in three layouts. Use the arrows in the bottom bar (or ← →) to switch. All three share the same sample data: Ola has an open Contract with two days still waiting for approval; Tymek has no open Contract. Everything responds. To build a Contract, pick Tymek and tap “Create a Contract”: dates, Tasks, price per gwiazdka and Weekly bonus in one form. You can also edit Ola's Contract, close and pay it out, and start the next one. Children, dates, rates and balances are sample data.

What to react to

  1. Layout: which one fits? A puts each child on their own page with pop-up sheets. B draws each child's Contracts as a timeline and opens full pages. C shows both children on one desk and does everything inline, with the payout as a receipt. Mixing is fine.
  2. Building a Contract: create one for Tymek. Is one form with dates, Tasks, price and bonus right? Tasks can still be changed while the Contract is open (tap Edit); price and bonus can't.
  3. Close & pay out: close Ola's Contract. Is the pending-days warning clear, and is it right to approve from there?
  4. The amount: does it show enough (Stars from Tasks, Weekly bonuses, rate) before you confirm?
  5. Start next contract: it starts the next School day, with the same rate and bonus, until the end of that semester. Right defaults?
  6. Create for Tymek: the start date can't be in the past here (earliest is today). Is that right, or should a Contract be allowed to start earlier?
  7. Closing early: the Contract's end date becomes the closing day. Try it, then switch the moment to 1 February to see a Contract whose end date has passed but isn't paid out yet.
  8. Past Contracts: how much of each old Payout you want to see.
